## Featured Work
 
### AI-Powered Clinical Lab Review Pipeline
 
Designed and led the build of a self-hosted LLM pipeline that automated health coach lab reviews against clinical guidelines, in a HIPAA-regulated environment.
 
| Metric | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Throughput** | 400%+ improvement, from ~10 patient reviews per coach per day to 50+ |
| **Turnaround** | Under 5 minutes, down from a 45-minute manual baseline |
| **Clinical accuracy** | 87% of AI-generated recommendations approved without modification |
| **Reliability** | 99.3% uptime across the MVP period |
| **Compliance** | PHI and PII de-identified at the pipeline boundary, never transmitted to the model |
 
Self-hosted Llama 2 with retrieval-augmented generation over AMA clinical standards. Event-sourced on GCP Pub/Sub, containerized on GKE, with VPC Service Controls, CMEK encryption, and full audit logging. Delivered across four phase-gated cycles with three engineers and one QA.
 
> **[Read the full case study →](https://github.com/five07/five07/blob/main/CaseStudy.md)**
> Covers stakeholder alignment, the technology-readiness evaluation, architecture decisions and their rationale, HIPAA compliance design, phased delivery, and lifecycle support.
